The menus

KeyWhat it does
Alt or F10select the menu bar, or leave it
Alt+F, E, R, S, T, Hopen File, Edit, Run, Settings, Tools or Help
Left, Rightmove along the bar, or to the menu beside the open one
Up, Downmove between the items; on the bar, open the menu
Enterchoose the highlighted item
An underlined letteropen that menu, or choose that item
Escapeclose the menu, or leave the bar

An item with an arrow at its right opens a submenu: Right, Enter or its letter open it, Left or Escape close just the submenu, and the keys above work in it. While a menu is open the shortcuts wait. A click outside the menus closes them and does nothing else. On macOS the menus are in the screen menu bar.

The registers

KeyWhat it does

Enterapply the value
Escapeleave the field as it was
